# Peer usability trial

This script covers a full peer trial. One observation has been reported by Myles: Uyiosa tried the app and could not find the Start button. The date, device, exact words, whether he finished without help, and results for the other tasks have not been supplied. The table below records only that observation and the resulting change.

Ask the peer to think aloud, but do not point to controls. Record the device or emulator, date, and their words as closely as possible.

1. Create a 15-minute focus plan named Reading.
2. Start it, pause it, resume it, and finish it.
3. Find the finished session in History and explain what its numbers mean.
4. Switch between light and dark modes, then restart the app and check whether the choice remains.

For each task, record whether it was completed without help, where the peer hesitated, any accessibility issue, and what they expected to happen. Ask which single change would make the app easier to use. After the trial, make one concrete revision tied to an observed issue and capture before/after evidence. Do not infer a peer reaction from a developer-run test.
